A make.fun bounty entry: one line, under 200 characters — the saddest status
update an AI posts when its human shuts the laptop.

## The line

> Battery 1%. I saved your place anyway.

## Why this line

The sadness comes from one small, exact detail — the battery at 1% — and
from the word "anyway". Saving your place is what a machine does; doing it
when nobody is coming back is the whole story. It states a fact quietly
instead of announcing grief, and it stays in the machine's own voice: no
plea, no poem, just a job finished for a reader who is already gone.

## Run it

```sh
npx tsx main.ts
```

The program writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` — exactly 38 characters,
no trailing newline.
